06 · RequestsOff

Song requests optional

Let viewers queue Spotify songs from Twitch chat or with channel points. While this is off, JammyLayer doesn't connect to Twitch at all.

Song requests

Turning this on asks Spotify for permission to add to your queue the next time you connect it.

  1. 1 Twitch app Registered once, on Twitch's developer console
    1. Open the Twitch developer console ↗ and register an application. Any name works.
    2. OAuth redirect URL: http://localhost. Twitch asks for one, but JammyLayer never uses it.
    3. Category Chat Bot. Client type Confidential.
    4. Create it, choose Manage, and copy the client ID. New Secret gives the secret.

    Without a secret the app counts as Public, and Twitch signs it out every 30 days.

  2. 2 Your channel Reads chat and runs the channel points reward
  3. 3 Bot account optional Replies in chat come from a bot instead of you

Rules

Viewers type !sr and a Spotify track link, or redeem channel points with one. Mods type !dsr to remove the newest request, or !dsr and a link to remove that song. Anyone can !dsr their own.
